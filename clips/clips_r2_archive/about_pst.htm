Production Systems Technologies, Inc








 

 

  
 

 

Production Systems Technologies (PST) is one of the most experienced suppliers of high-performance rule-based systems. A spin-off of Carnegie-Mellon University, PST was founded in 1983 to develop and market state of the art rule-based tools. The founder and chief scientist of Production Systems Technologies is Dr. Charles Forgy, inventor of the Rete algorithm, and the principal designer of OPS5, the language in which the first commercially successful expert system was written. PST has a proven track record of providing practical tools for solving real world problems. Major companies in North America, Europe, and Japan have used PST tools to develop and field operational rule-based systems for over two decades.
